# InFiles for Mac

**Private AI for your documents.** Ask questions about your PDFs, spreadsheets and scanned receipts and get answers with sources, 100% offline on your Mac.

Website: [infiles.app](https://infiles.app)

## Download
Get the latest version from [Releases](https://github.com/aicraftsmen/infiles_public/releases/latest), open `InFiles-<version>.dmg` and drag **InFiles** into **Applications**.

The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Free trial and license
- The free trial includes **10 questions** in total, across any folders and files.
- A **lifetime license is $49 USD**, one time. See [pricing](https://infiles.app/pricing/).
- To activate, open InFiles, click the license badge in the sidebar (or **Settings, License**) and enter your key.
- To move to a new Mac, use **Release license from this Mac** in Settings, then activate on the new Mac.

## Requirements
- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later)
- macOS 13 Ventura or later
- 8 GB memory (16 GB for the most accurate model)
- 1 to 5 GB of free disk space for the AI model
- An internet connection on first launch, to download the AI model and start the trial

## Privacy
Your documents, questions and answers never leave your Mac. The app goes online only to download its AI model, to start the trial and activate a license, and for a small license check. Details: [infiles.app/privacy.html](https://infiles.app/privacy.html).
